FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does a brand strategist do?

Defines how a company wants to be perceived and organizes everything to make it happen: positioning, messaging, visual identity and experiences.

What's the difference between brand strategy and visual identity?

Strategy defines what the brand stands for and who it speaks to. Visual identity translates that into shape, color and typography.

Do you work with companies from any industry and location?

Yes. I work with companies from any industry, through online meetings and in person when it makes sense. I'm based in Cascavel, Paraná, Brazil.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. In our first conversation I understand where your brand stands and give you a clear timeline.
