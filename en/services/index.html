FAQ

About the services

What is a brand diagnosis?

It's an analysis of where your brand stands today: how clients and the team perceive it, what it communicates, how it compares to competitors and where it's missing opportunities. It's the basis for any brand decision.

What are brand territories?

They're the themes and spaces where the brand can act with legitimacy. They guide content, events, sponsorships and partnerships, and keep the brand from talking about everything while being remembered for nothing.

What is brand architecture?

It's how a company organizes its brands, products and units. It defines what carries the main name, what gets its own name and how everything connects, so the company can grow without confusing clients.

Do you design just the logo?

Yes, as long as there's strategy behind it. If the company only needs the logo as its visual front, that's fine. It always comes from understanding the business and the brand.

Do you produce the events?

No. I build the experience and lead the creative direction, working with your team and the producers. They handle execution, guided by that direction.

Do I need to hire every service?

No. Each service works on its own. But since visual identity comes from strategy, many projects combine those two fronts.
